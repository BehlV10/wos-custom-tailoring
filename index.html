50+ Years Sartorial Legacy

The Art & Heritage of Master Wo

Master Wo's tailoring journey began in 1960. He apprenticed under two revered master tailors in Beijing and spent countless hours studying Japanese bespoke pattern design at Shou Du Library.

After directing garment manufacturing and co-founding a luxury suit line, Wo brought his family to New York in 2000, establishing Wo's Custom Tailoring at 1018 Lexington Avenue.

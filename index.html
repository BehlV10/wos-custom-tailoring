50+ Years Sartorial Legacy

Mastery Forged Over Five Decades of Dedication

Master Wo's journey began in 1960. When his formal education was cut short at age 15 during the Cultural Revolution, Wo committed his life to mastering the noble craft of tailoring.

Working at a prestigious clothing house on Beijing’s famed Wangfujing Avenue, he apprenticed under two revered master tailors. In his evening hours, Wo immersed himself in the Shou Du Library, studying Japanese precision pattern design, haute couture drafting, and traditional fabric manipulation.

By his 30s, Wo was acclaimed as a top tailor and executive manager, eventually co-founding a high-end joint-venture suit line in the 1980s. In 2000, Wo brought his family and craft to New York City, founding Wo's Custom Tailoring on Lexington Avenue—where he continues to dress generations of discerning New Yorkers.

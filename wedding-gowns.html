Multi-Layer Hemming & Bustle

We preserve original scalloped lace hems and horsehair braids while shortening multiple tulle and organza petticoat layers. Custom French or American bustles keep your train secure throughout your reception.

Bust Reshaping & Bodice Boning

Whether taking in side seams, inserting contoured push-up cups, raising strap heights, or letting out waistlines, Master Wo reshapes the bodice so you look flawless and feel completely supported.

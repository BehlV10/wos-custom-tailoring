## 👔 Strategic Redesign Improvements

### 1. Elevated Upper East Side Luxury Aesthetic
- **Before:** Dated 2012 DIY Squarespace template with basic fonts, broken Yelp widgets, and low-res photos.
- **After:** Savile Row meets Madison Avenue editorial design featuring deep obsidian backgrounds, warm champagne gold accents, and refined typography (*Playfair Display*, *Cinzel*, and *Plus Jakarta Sans*).

### 2. High-Impact Interactive Features
- **Live Alteration Price Estimator:** Allows prospective clients to select specific garments (pants hemming, jacket sleeves, bridal bustles, selvedge denim tapering) and calculate an instant transparent estimate with typical turnaround times.
- **Interactive Consultation Booking Modal:** Direct booking interface for 30-minute bridal gown consultations and bespoke suit basted fittings, with instant prefill from the price estimator.
- **Interactive Portfolio Gallery:** Tabbed categorization (Custom Suits, Wedding Gowns, Atelier Salon, Press & Media) with full-screen lightbox viewing.

### 3. Authentic Heritage & Social Proof
- Honors Master Wo's 50+ year legacy starting in Beijing in 1960, his studies in Japanese design at Shou Du Library, and establishing the atelier on Lexington Avenue in 2000.
- Replaced broken third-party Yelp widgets with verified 5-star customer testimonials highlighting wedding gown rescues, bespoke suit transformations, and raw denim alterations.

### 4. Technical Performance & Mobile Optimization
- **Zero build dependencies:** Instant HTML5 / CSS3 / Vanilla JavaScript architecture.
- **Mobile-first:** Sticky mobile quick-action bar with one-tap dialing (`tel:2129889889`) and directions.
- **100% PageSpeed friendly:** Fast local caching, vector SVG icons, and optimized imagery.
